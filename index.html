    Before you post

    Four lines from the identity guideline, for every image that leaves these tools.

    Guideline v0.2

    Always

    1. 4:5 vertical for the feed, 9:16 for stories and reels. Subject on the central axis.
    2. Visible grain, even on product images.
    3. One hard light (flash, low sun) or total mist.
    4. In motion, the logo only appears at the end, in Signal green.

    Never

    1. Blue sky, saturated colours, postcard panoramas.
    2. More than one accent colour: green is the only one, at 4 % of the image at most.
    3. Green text on a light background. Green goes as a flat area, a line, or on black.
    4. The logo stamped in a corner like a watermark.
